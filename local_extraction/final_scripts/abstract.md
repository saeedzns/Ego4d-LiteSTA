# Abstract

Short-Term Object Interaction Anticipation (STA) on Ego4D video demands last-frame spatial precision, semantic grounding (verb/noun), and timing (time-to-contact) under tight compute budgets. Ego4D-LiteSTA delivers a modular, reproducible three-track pipeline tuned for Colab-scale resources: (1) **Track A** couples a recall-first last-frame detector with a lightweight reasoning head to emit top-K candidates and head manifests; (2) **Track B** adds Frame-Guided Temporal Pooling and dual image↔video cross-attention using pre-extracted tokens, yielding a compact multi-task head (next-active, noun/verb, TTC) with ~27x faster training when tokens are cached; (3) **Track C** applies training-free Rollout-Guided Token Pruning (RGTP) at inference, routinely trimming 35–50% latency with sub-0.5 mAP drop.

The system prioritizes candidate recall@K, transparent manifests, and end-to-end reproducibility across detector sweeps, fusion ablations, and pruning curves on Ego4D-STA v2. Experiments show that lightweight fusion raises N+V and N+δ metrics with minimal overhead, while RGTP preserves accuracy even under aggressive pruning, making the approach practical for real-time AR or robot-assist settings where latency and memory are constrained. Together, the three tracks form a Pareto-efficient recipe that balances accuracy and efficiency, and the documented workflow invites straightforward replication, extension to new backbones, and deployment on modest hardware.
